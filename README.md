# Kasirajan Meenatchisundaram — Senior Agronomist Portfolio

A production-quality, multi-page professional portfolio website for **Kasirajan Meenatchisundaram**, Senior Agronomist & Farm Management Professional, showcasing over 30 years of practical commercial agriculture, farm development, protected cultivation, contract farming, and sustainable agricultural leadership in India and the Sultanate of Oman.

---

## 🌾 Design System & Creative Direction

- **Forest Green (`#183D2B`)**: Primary brand anchor, symbolizing deep agricultural heritage and leadership.
- **Sage Green (`#65865A`)**: Balanced tone for highlights, badges, and secondary elements.
- **Leaf Green (`#A3B987`)**: Vibrant accent for yield stats, growth metrics, and interactive elements.
- **Warm Cream (`#F5F3E9`)**: Organic paper texture and clean editorial background.
- **Earth Brown (`#9A795B`)**: Earthy soil tone used for arid zone & international highlights.
- **White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Card surfaces and contrast typography.
- **Typography**: *Playfair Display* for serif editorial headings, *Inter* for modern high-legibility body prose.
